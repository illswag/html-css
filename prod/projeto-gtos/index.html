gt-os 1: Baseline.

by GT.

gt-os doesn't just run on your hardware—it listens to it.

from the silicon up with a zero-abstraction kernel, gt-os profiles every thread, cache line, and voltage curve in real time, reallocating resources at nanosecond intervals to squeeze out every unused cycle.

its adaptive scheduler learns the rhythm of your workload, whether that's rendering a feature film, training a neural net, or running a thousand background services, and reshapes itself around it rather than forcing your hardware into someone else's assumptions.
